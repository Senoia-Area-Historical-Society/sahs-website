## 1. Announcement — Mon Oct 5, 9:00 AM

**Facebook**
This Thursday, October 8: Caring for Your Family Treasures with Dr. Mark Janzen of the University of West Georgia.

Old photographs, letters, quilts and keepsakes tell your family's story. Dr. Janzen will show you how to protect and preserve the ones that matter most to you. Bring a family heirloom or keepsake, and he will help you work out how best to care for it.
